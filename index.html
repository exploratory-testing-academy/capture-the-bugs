Test your exploratory testing skills

How it works

  1. Pick a target application below
  2. Explore it thoroughly — try different inputs, poke around, look at everything
  3. For each bug you notice, describe it in the report panel
  4. When you think you've found everything, submit for evaluation

An AI model running in your browser will match your reports against the known bug list and score your session, so the scoring itself happens on your machine.

Sessions are recorded. The inputs you try and the findings you write are saved to a database so this exercise can be studied and improved. There is no name, login or e-mail involved — your session carries only a random code, shown while you work. Share that code if you want your session looked at; keep it to yourself and nobody can connect the session to you.
